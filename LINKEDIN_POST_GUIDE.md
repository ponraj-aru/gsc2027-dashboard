*(Simply copy the text below into your LinkedIn post and attach the 4:5 Portrait image)*

🌍 Is your institution represented at GSC 2027?

Researchers, academic leaders, and policymakers worldwide are converging on Groningen to evaluate 15 years of SDG implementation and co-create the post-2030 sustainability roadmap.

🌿 Theme 1: SDGs Beyond 2030 — 41 Nations Engaged
💧 Theme 2: Resilience Across Systems — 32 Nations Engaged

🌍 Continental Representation:
• Europe: 18 Nations
• Asia & Middle East: 27 Nations
• Africa: 16 Nations
• Americas: 8 Nations

We warmly invite partner universities, global consortia, and research institutes to participate and present.

🔗 Learn more & submit your proposals: rug.nl/sustainability-conference-2027
📍 Zernike Campus, Groningen, The Netherlands | April 05–10, 2027

#Sustainability #GSC2027 #SDGs #Post2030 #UniversityOfGroningen #HigherEducation #GlobalResearch #ClimateAction #AIforGood #PlanetaryHealth

## 🏷️ Recommended Institutions & Organizations to Tag on LinkedIn

To maximize virality and institutional reach, tag relevant partner universities and global networks using `@`:

### 1. Host & European Alliances:
- `@University of Groningen` / `@Rijksuniversiteit Groningen`
- `@ENLIGHT European University` (The 9-university alliance including Ghent, Uppsala, Göttingen, Bordeaux, Tartu, Galway, Comenius, Basque Country)
- `@Coimbra Group`
- `@The Guild of European Research-Intensive Universities`
- `@EUA - European University Association`

### 2. Global Networks & Multilaterals:
- `@UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN)`
- `@United Nations Academic Impact (UNAI)`
- `@UNESCO`
- `@Future Earth`
- `@International Science Council (ISC)`

### 3. Key Participating Regions to Tag:
- **Europe:** `@TU Delft`, `@Wageningen University & Research`, `@Utrecht University`, `@ETH Zürich`, `@Technical University of Munich`, `@University of Oxford`, `@University of Cambridge`.
- **Asia:** `@National University of Singapore`, `@Tsinghua University`, `@Peking University`, `@Indian Institute of Technology`, `@Universitas Indonesia`, `@Universitas Gadjah Mada`.
- **Africa:** `@University of Cape Town`, `@University of the Witwatersrand`, `@University of Ghana`, `@University of Nairobi`, `@ARUA - African Research Universities Alliance`.
- **Americas:** `@Harvard University`, `@Columbia University Climate School`, `@Universidade de São Paulo`, `@UNAM`, `@University of Toronto`.
- **Oceania:** `@University of Melbourne`, `@Australian National University`, `@University of Auckland`.
